What these captures show

All nine images are lossless, native 640 × 480 PC captures. Watch and matrix show real France 24 streams decoded by libmpv through App.finish_player, App.update and App.draw. Settings and quality use that same single-feed session, continuing behind opaque menus. Browser and installer screens are scripted fixtures with authentic channel names and sample cards, not a downloaded world catalog or a completed installation.

The capture run accessed the network and decoded video; this website serves only local media and makes no stream or external-media requests. No handheld, controller, audio device or personal saved state was accessed. These are PC playback captures, not performance or audio verification. Historical hardware verification dated 2026-10-19 remains separate in the build notes.

Broadcast frames and station marks: France 24 / France Medias Monde and the respective rights holders. Broadcast content is not covered by the site's MIT license; no broadcaster endorsement is implied. See NOTICE.md for sources and rights information.

Regeneration and capture records

The website tool dev/render_tv_captures.py --capture-streams --source-dir /path/to/melkor227-tv requires explicit network/playback opt-in and compatible host SDL2/SDL2_ttf and libmpv libraries, not an FFmpeg executable. --validate-only prints a pure plan without loading the app or native libraries or accessing the network. The capture provenance record includes source URLs, timestamps, observed frame counts, source formats and source/image hashes; the screenshot manifest maps the gallery images.
