227 TV / Overview

227 TVLive TV on a globe.

Spin the world atlas with the controller, pick a country and watch its live stations. No app account or app backend is required: channel lists need internet access, and playback connects to broadcasters' stream servers. It shares the handheld's 227 menu with 227 AI, but works without it.

  • Python + SDL2
  • Software libmpv
  • Controller-first

01 / HOW IT WORKSNATIVE APP / NOT A BROWSER DEMO

A native app.
A spinning atlas.

227 TV descends from the Melkor 227 plugin in my Omarchy bar: the desktop station atlas where thousands of live TV stations ride a rotatable globe. This is a controller-first Python/SDL2 source port of that plugin for the R36S, not a bit-for-bit reproduction of its Qt/QML version. The handheld UI renders with SDL software rasterization and libmpv's software RGBA API: no Qt, no external player window and no GPU video surface.

Each country's channel list is downloaded only when you open it, with a separate 24-hour cache. Favorites and Recent come from saved snapshots without downloading the world list. Startup loads only the local map and saved preferences.

Built for the KMS display.

The app owns the handheld screen exclusively. It records the EmulationStation ancestor that launched it and refuses background or orphaned KMSDRM launches to avoid competing for rendering and controller input. Launch it from 227 > 227 TV, never with nohup or a background service.

The historical hardware verification on 2026-10-19 covered globe and country rendering, search, local video, and live France 24 with active ALSA audio. These PC captures are not new device tests. High-resolution streams can be slow, and availability depends on the broadcaster. Read the recorded verification and its limits.
